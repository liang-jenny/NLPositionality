# Dataset Licensing

NLPositionality combines annotations and research data collected through LabintheWild with material from existing source datasets. Different components therefore have different licenses.

## NLPositionality-created data

The annotations and associated research data collected through LabintheWild as part of NLPositionality are released under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.

This license applies only to rights held by the NLPositionality licensors. It does not replace or override licenses that apply to third-party material included in the released files.

## Third-party source material

- **Social Chemistry 101:** Source material from Social Chemistry 101 remains licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See the [Social Chemistry 101 repository](https://github.com/mbforbes/social-chemistry-101).
- **DynaHate:** Source material from the Dynamically Generated Hate Speech Dataset (DynaHate) remains licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the [DynaHate repository](https://github.com/bvidgen/Dynamically-Generated-Hate-Speech-Dataset).

If you reuse NLPositionality, please comply with the license applicable to each component you use.
